Tag Tag: Playground Blitz Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

Tag Tag: Playground Blitz does not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal information.

The game is designed for offline single-player play. It does not include accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, cloud saves, or multiplayer servers.

If future versions add online services, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud features, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety answers will be updated before release.

Questions can be sent through the GitHub repository at github.com/ibsam588-lgtm/tag-tag.
