Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Tag Tag: Playground Blitz is a game prototype. It does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send gameplay data to a server.

Local Data

The in-game idea form stores submitted ideas only in the browser or app storage on your device. This data stays on your device and can be cleared by clearing the app or browser storage.

Children

The game is designed as a family-friendly arcade game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future Services

If analytics, ads, accounts, leaderboards, cloud saves, or multiplayer services are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository: github.com/ibsam588-lgtm/tag-tag.
